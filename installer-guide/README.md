# Installing the driver

Congrats! You've successfully got to the part where you might be scratching your head going, "Well, how do I actually use it?"
Fear not, little moth.. -intosher. You've got two (2) options on how to proceed.

### Choices

Depending on which OS you're on, see your specific section on making the USB:

* [Windows Users](../installer-guide/windows-install.md)
  * For those who have yet to install macOS
  * EFI is made for you, if you so wish, but can be made with your own OpenCore EFI
* [macOS users](../installer-guide/mac-install.md)
  * For those that already have a working system on Sequoia (or Tahoe)
  * Relies on you actually being on Sequoia or Tahoe. The driver is very unlikely to work outside of those two.
  * Driver is installed for you via the 1401 app
* Linux users
  * Sorry, there's no support right now for Linux. You will have to make your own EFI following the [Dortania OpenCore Guide](https://dortania.github.io/OpenCore-Install-Guide/), and then follow the [macOS Users](../installer-guide/mac-install.md) guide to install the driver.
